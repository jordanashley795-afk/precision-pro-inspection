Job Summary

PrecisionPro Inspection Co. LLC is seeking a dependable, detail-oriented Field Inspector to conduct on-site residential, commercial, and mortgage compliance inspections. In this role, you will travel to assigned properties within a designated local radius to document physical conditions, confirm occupancy status, and execute specialized loss control or inventory audits.

This is a highly autonomous, field-based position ideal for self-motivated individuals looking for flexible, production-based work.

Key Responsibilities

  • Property Inspections: Complete physical exterior and interior walk-throughs of residential, commercial, and default/foreclosure real estate properties.
  • Occupancy Verification: Conduct rapid site visits to confirm property occupancy status, verify tenant details, or document property vacancy/abandonment.
  • Risk & Loss Control Assessments: Inspect structural elements (roofing, electrical panels, terrain hazards) and commercial layouts to spot liabilities, safety issues, or fire risks.
  • Asset & Inventory Tracking: Perform counts and physical evaluations on specialty commercial equipment, retail storage setups, or grounds landscaping parameters.
  • Digital Data Collection: Take high-quality, sequential photographs from required angles and complete structured electronic forms using mobile reporting software on-site.

Physical Requirements

  • Comfortable walking large commercial grids and navigating uneven terrain
  • Capable of occasionally using basic tools (measuring wheels, ladders, steps)
  • Willingness to work in all seasons and weather

Preferred Experience

  • Property preservation background
  • Insurance adjusting or loss control experience
  • Construction or real estate knowledge
  • Mystery shopping or retail auditing experience
Note: Prior experience is a major plus but not required. We're looking for detail-oriented, reliable individuals who can work independently and take pride in thorough documentation.
